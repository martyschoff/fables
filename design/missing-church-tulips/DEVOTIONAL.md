# Devotional: The Gift That Honors God

© Martin Schoffstall. End matter for *The Missing Church Tulips* (Martin t434u). Text supplied by Martin; assembled unchanged.

## Scripture Reading
- Proverbs 15:3 — "The eyes of the Lord are everywhere, keeping watch on the wicked and the good."
- Galatians 6:7 — "Do not be deceived: God cannot be mocked. A man reaps what he sows."

## The Big Idea
We cannot do a right thing by making a wrong choice. God sees our hearts, and true blessings come when we honor Him first.

## The Message: Flowers, Foiled Plans, and a Patient Father

## Family Discussion Questions
1. **The "Secret" Choice** — Leo was tempted because he thought nobody was watching. How does knowing God is always with you change that?
2. **Good Intentions, Wrong Actions** — Why is it still wrong to steal, even for a good cause?
3. **True Blessings** — Why does a gift earned through hard work and honesty feel better than something taken dishonestly?

## Closing Prayer
Dear Heavenly Father, Thank You for always keeping watch over me, not to catch me making mistakes, but because You love me and care about my heart. Help me remember that I can never hide my choices from You. Give me the wisdom to know that a good goal should always be reached in a righteous way. Teach me to respect Your house, Your people, and the things dedicated to You. Help me to honor my parents and my friends with a pure and honest heart. Amen.

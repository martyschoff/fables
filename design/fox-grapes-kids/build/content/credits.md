Fern and the High Grapes
A new retelling of The Fox and the Grapes.

Source: "The Fox and the Grapes," The Fables of Phaedrus, Book IV, translated by Henry T. Riley and Christopher Smart (Project Gutenberg eBook #25512), archived at github.com/martyschoff/fables.

This story is a NEW adaptation for young readers, not the words of Phaedrus, Riley, or Smart. Fern's pause, honest admission, prayer, and reconsideration are new material added in this adaptation.

Scripture quotations are from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB). The BSB has been dedicated to the public domain.

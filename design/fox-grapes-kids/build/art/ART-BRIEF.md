# ART-BRIEF — Fern and the High Grapes (extracted from DESIGN.md @ 93bd8d1 §5–6; Martin t428u)
Style: warm watercolor + colored pencil, soft edges, expressive animal posture, lightly textured paper.
Palette: russet fox, plum grapes, sage leaves, golden grass, creek blue, warm cream.
Fern: female red fox, quadrupedal, no clothing, consistent proportions. Tall grape arbor at vineyard edge; fixed fruit height.
Setting: fictional Finger Lakes NY family vineyard, late summer; red barn, split-rail fence, gravel lane, apple basket, blue hills.
Avoid: sinister grapes, mocking birds, injury humor, frightening teeth, magical divine light, final reward bunch; grapes never proven sour.
Dedication: upper-left diagonal "for Immy" on cover + title/dedication page (Martin t428u).

Format: 8.5" sq pages; spreads 17x8.5 (2:1) + 0.125" bleed; leave text space, keep gutter clear.

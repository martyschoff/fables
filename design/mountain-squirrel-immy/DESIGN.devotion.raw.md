# Devotional: Honoring God’s Good Design

APPROVED design context (Martin t461u, mountain lock t464u). © Martin Schoffstall. End matter for *Immy and Hunter Mountain: A Catskills Fable*.

## Scripture Reading
- Psalm 139:14 (BSB) — I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Marvelous are Your works, and I know this very well.
- 1 Corinthians 12:18-20 (BSB) — 18 But in fact, God has arranged the members of the body, every one of them, according to His design. 19 If they were all one part, where would the body be? 20 As it is, there are many parts, but one body.

## The Big Idea
God made us with care. We honor His design when we thank Him for our gifts and value the gifts He gives others.

## The Message
Hunter Mountain could carry a forest, but Immy could crack a nut! Being bigger did not make Hunter Mountain more important. Being smaller did not make Immy less valuable. Hunter Mountain needed to let go of pride, and Immy could be glad for how God made her. Each could appreciate the other’s gifts.

Sometimes we boast about what we can do. Sometimes we feel small or wish we were someone else. God’s Word helps us answer both feelings with gratitude. In the church, the Body of Christ, God gives His people different gifts so they can serve together. Just as our hands and feet do different jobs, we can help in different ways. We can thank God for our own gifts and cheer for someone else’s!

## Family Discussion Questions
1. **Different Gifts** — What could Immy do? What could Hunter Mountain do?
2. **A Thankful Heart** — What is something about the way God made you that you can thank Him for?
3. **Enjoying Others’ Gifts** — When someone can do something you cannot, how can you encourage them?
4. **Serving Together** — What is one way our family can help someone this week?

## Closing Prayer
Dear God, thank You for making us with care. Forgive us when we boast or forget that Your design is good. Help us use our gifts to serve, enjoy the gifts of others, and love one another as members of the Body of Christ. Amen.

```text
The mountain and the squirrel
Had a quarrel;
And the former called the latter 'Little Prig.'
Bun replied,
'You are doubtless very big;
But all sorts of things and weather
Must be taken in together,
To make up a year
And a sphere.
And I think it no disgrace
To occupy my place.
If I'm not so large as you,
You are not so small as I,
And not half so spry.
I'll not deny you make
A very pretty squirrel track;
Talents differ; all is well and wisely put;
If I cannot carry forests on my back,
Neither can you crack a nut.'
```
